# Parts Manager — changelog

Released versions only.

## 1.0 - first release

First public release. Parts Manager adds an app to the tablet and the office computer that shows what's in your warehouse storage and buys parts straight into it at normal store prices.

- See your balance, reputation level, fame level and free storage, and how many complete cars the parts in storage can build.
- Buy complete cars: pick a kit and a number of cars, and get that many full sets. Sport and Race kits need the Up 2 You DLC and unlock with fame. Body parts and fluids per car are optional.
- Buy any single part for any number of cars: brake calipers for 2 cars buys 8.
- Auto-stock keeps a set number of complete cars of each kit in storage, and never spends below the balance you set. Auto Fuses keeps every fuse size in stock.
- A brand-new career starts with auto-stock paused, so the mod never spends a new player's money without being asked.
- A purchase you can't afford, or that won't fit in storage, is refused before anything is charged. The game's error sound plays and the app says why.
- Report a Bug and Request a Feature buttons in the app's header.
